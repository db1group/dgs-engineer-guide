# DevOps Seniority Levels

At DB1, we believe excellence in DevOps is essential to delivering high-quality software with efficiency, security, and scalability. DevOps professionals play a strategic role in integrating development, operations, and business, promoting a culture of collaboration, automation, and continuous improvement.

The following are the expected DevOps seniority levels, focusing on key skills, behaviors, and responsibilities.

---

## **DevOps Trainee**

- **You are beginning your DevOps journey or have little practical experience.**
  - You are in the organization to learn the fundamentals of DevOps culture, including CI/CD, IaC (Infrastructure as Code), and monitoring.
  - You are curious and willing to explore tools such as **Git**, **Docker**, **Jenkins**, **Terraform**, and other emerging technologies.
  - You learn to work in collaborative environments and understand how software deliveries affect the business.
  - You accept **feedback as an opportunity to grow** and seek to learn from more experienced colleagues.
  - You actively participate in training and workshops to consolidate your knowledge.

---

## **DevOps Junior**

### **Technical Skills:**
- Configures basic CI/CD pipelines using tools such as Jenkins, GitLab CI, or GitHub Actions.
- Creates and manages containers with Docker and Docker Compose.
- Implements automation scripts for repetitive tasks using Bash, Python, or PowerShell.
- Understands **Infrastructure as Code (IaC)** principles and uses tools such as Terraform or AWS CloudFormation to provision basic resources.
- Performs basic monitoring with tools such as Prometheus, Grafana, or ELK Stack.

### **Behavior:**
- Contributes to small projects or modules with a commitment to quality and delivery.
- Shows interest in learning about security, process optimization, and best practices.

### **Expected Experience:**
- Between **1 and 2 years**. It is expected that you will not take the full two years to reach the next level.

---

## **DevOps Mid-Level**

### **Technical Skills:**
- Designs robust, scalable CI/CD pipelines that integrate automated tests, static code analysis, and security.
- Implements complete Infrastructure as Code (IaC) solutions, ensuring reproducibility and consistency across environments.
- Configures and maintains Kubernetes clusters for production application deployments.
- Understands **observability** and implements advanced monitoring solutions with alerts and custom dashboards.
- Applies **DevSecOps** practices by integrating security from the beginning of the pipeline.

### **Behavior:**
- Works autonomously and makes technical decisions aligned with business objectives.
- Collaborates actively with development and operations teams, promoting quality and shared responsibility.
- Identifies process bottlenecks and proposes improvements to increase efficiency.

### **Expected Experience:**
- Between **2 and 4 years**. Must demonstrate autonomy and advanced skills to progress to the next level.

---

## **DevOps Senior**

### **Technical Skills:**
- Has **deep theoretical and practical knowledge** of DevOps, including distributed systems architecture, advanced automation, and security.
- Designs and implements resilient, scalable infrastructure architectures using cloud-native services (AWS, Azure, GCP, OCI) and open-source tools.
- Solves complex performance, scalability, and security problems while ensuring high system availability.
- Influences strategic technical decisions and aligns them with business objectives.

### **Behavior:**
- Acts as a mentor and technical guide, supporting the team's growth.
- Inspires confidence by making data- and metrics-based decisions.
- Understands how infrastructure decisions affect cost and operational efficiency.

### **Expected Experience:**
- At least **4 years** working in the field.

---

## **DevOps Lead**

### **Technical Skills:**
- Owns the DevOps strategy for a team or organization:
  - Defines and implements standards for automation, monitoring, and security.
  - Ensures DevOps practices align with business goals.
- Leads cultural transformation initiatives and promotes agile methodologies and collaborative practices.
- Manages critical infrastructure crises, coordinating efforts to minimize impact and prevent future incidents.
- Sees the whole picture and connects infrastructure areas such as automation, observability, security, and governance.

### **Behavior:**
- Moves comfortably between technical subjects, from system architecture to implementation details.
- Inspires and develops team talent, creating an environment of continuous learning.
- Clearly communicates to customers and stakeholders how infrastructure decisions affect business outcomes.

### **Expected Experience:**
- At least **5 years**.

---

These levels are a reference for career development and aligning expectations. We know each person's growth is unique and exceptions exist, but this guide can support the development of DevOps professionals within **DB1**.
