# FinOps Best Practices

Efficient cost management is essential to maximize infrastructure ROI. The following are best practices:

---

### **1. Spend Identification**
Analyze invoices to identify:
- Major cost drivers (services or regions with high spending).
- Idle or underutilized resources.

Document clear optimization recommendations, such as shutting down or restructuring resources.

---

### **2. Financial Alerts**
Configure alerts to notify teams when:
- Spending exceeds defined limits.
- Spending approaches critical budgets.

Implement dashboards to track daily/weekly spending, organized by cost center whenever possible.

---

### **3. Cost-Saving Policies**
Adopt policies to reduce unnecessary costs:
- Automatically shut down non-essential resources outside business hours.
- Optimize data storage based on access frequency (e.g., move older data to cheaper tiers).

---

### **4. Multi-Cloud Strategies**
Compare prices across cloud providers to choose the best value for specific services.  
Adopt serverless approaches whenever possible, migrating applications to services such as:
- **AWS Lambda**
- **Azure Functions**

These strategies help reduce fixed costs and increase operational efficiency.
