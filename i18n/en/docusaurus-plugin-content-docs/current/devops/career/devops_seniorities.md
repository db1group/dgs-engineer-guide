# DevOps Seniority Levels

At DB1, we believe that excellence in DevOps is fundamental to delivering high-quality software, ensuring efficiency, security and scalability. DevOps professionals play a strategic role in the integration between development, operations and business, promoting a culture of collaboration, automation and continuous improvement.

Here are the expected seniority levels for DevOps, focusing on skills, behaviors and main responsibilities.

---

## **DevOps Trainee**

- **You are starting your journey in the DevOps world or have little practical experience.**
  - You are in the organization to learn the fundamentals of DevOps culture, including practices such as CI/CD, IaC (Infrastructure as Code) and monitoring.
  - Shows curiosity and willingness to explore tools such as **Git**, **Docker**, **Jenkins**, **Terraform** and other emerging technologies.
  - Learns to work in collaborative environments, understanding how software deliveries impact the business.
  - Accepts **feedback as an opportunity for growth** and seeks to learn from the more experienced.
  - Actively participates in trainings and workshops to consolidate their knowledge.

---

## **DevOps Junior**

### **Technical Skills:**
- Configures basic CI/CD pipelines using tools such as Jenkins, GitLab CI or GitHub Actions.
- Creates and manages containers with Docker and Docker Compose.
- Implements automation scripts for repetitive tasks, using languages such as Bash, Python or PowerShell.
- Understands the principles of **Infrastructure as Code (IaC)** and uses tools such as Terraform or AWS CloudFormation to provision basic resources.
- Performs basic monitoring with tools such as Prometheus, Grafana or ELK Stack.

### **Behavior:**
- Contributes to small projects or modules, demonstrating commitment to quality and delivery.
- Shows interest in learning about security, process optimization and best practices.

### **Expected Experience:**
- Between **1 and 2 years**. It is expected that you will not take up to 2 years to reach the next level.

---

## **DevOps Mid-Level**

### **Technical Skills:**
- Designs robust and scalable CI/CD pipelines, integrating automated tests, static code analysis and security.
- Implements complete Infrastructure as Code (IaC) solutions, ensuring replicability and consistency across environments.
- Configures and maintains Kubernetes clusters for deploying applications in production.
- Understands the principles of **observability** and implements advanced monitoring solutions, with custom alerts and dashboards.
- Applies **DevSecOps** practices, integrating security from the beginning of the pipeline.

### **Behavior:**
- Works autonomously, making technical decisions aligned with business objectives.
- Actively collaborates with development and operations teams, promoting a culture of quality and shared responsibility.
- Identifies bottlenecks in processes and proposes improvements to increase efficiency.

### **Expected Experience:**
- Between **2 and 4 years**. Must demonstrate autonomy and advanced skills to advance to the next level.

---

## **DevOps Senior**

### **Technical Skills:**
- Has **deep theoretical and practical knowledge** in DevOps, including distributed systems architecture, advanced automation and security.
- Designs and implements resilient and scalable infrastructure architectures, using cloud-native services (AWS, Azure, GCP, OCI) and open source tools.
- Solves complex performance, scalability and security problems, ensuring high availability of systems.
- Influences strategic technical decisions, aligning them with business objectives.

### **Behavior:**
- Acts as a mentor and technical guide, helping the team evolve.
- Inspires confidence by making decisions based on data and metrics.
- Understands the impact of infrastructure decisions on cost and operational efficiency.

### **Expected Experience:**
- At least **4 years** working in the field.

---

## **DevOps Lead**

### **Technical Skills:**
- Is responsible for the DevOps strategy of the team or organization:
  - Defines and implements standards for automation, monitoring and security.
  - Ensures that DevOps practices are aligned with business goals.
- Leads cultural transformation initiatives, promoting the adoption of agile methodologies and collaboration practices.
- Manages critical infrastructure crises, coordinating efforts to minimize impacts and prevent future incidents.
- Sees the "whole" and connects different aspects of the infrastructure, such as automation, observability, security and governance.

### **Behavior:**
- Moves easily between technical subjects, from systems architecture to implementation details.
- Inspires and develops talent within the team, creating an environment of continuous learning.
- Communicates clearly to the customer and stakeholders how infrastructure decisions impact business results.

### **Expected Experience:**
- Minimum of **5 years**.

---

These levels serve as a reference to guide career development and align expectations. We know that evolution is unique for each person and that there are exceptions to the rules, but this guide can serve as a basis to support the growth of DevOps within **DB1**.
