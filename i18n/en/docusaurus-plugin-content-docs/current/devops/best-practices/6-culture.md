# DevOps Culture Best Practices

DevOps culture is the foundation for promoting collaboration, autonomy and continuous improvement. Below are the best practices to strengthen this culture:

---

### **1. Clear Communication**
Keep the team informed about changes in the delivery process.  
- Encourage continuous feedback between development and operations.  
- Ensure that all changes are communicated transparently.

---

### **2. Post-Mortem and Continuous Improvement**
Carry out detailed analyses after critical incidents:  
- Document the lessons learned.  
- Use these lessons to improve processes and avoid repeating errors.  

Encourage a culture of controlled experimentation, where tests and innovations can be carried out safely.

---

### **3. Full Autonomy**
Empower the team to operate autonomously:  
- Eliminate external dependencies.  
- Focus on business results, ensuring that each team has control over its deliveries.  

This approach increases efficiency and promotes a sense of collective ownership.
